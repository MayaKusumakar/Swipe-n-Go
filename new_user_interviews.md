## Improvements Made to the App 
# What you changed and Why 
**1. Clear "Leave Plan" button )**
- **What we heard:** In the first 5 interviews, people struggled to leave a plan if something came up and couldn't attend the event. Grace in interview 4 mentioned that she would haven't figured out because there were no clear buttons to leave a plan and was too much of a hassle.
- **What we changed:** We added a "leave plan" button in the events page so that a user can opt out in just one click.
- **Why:** This was an important change because people might come up with someting urgent or decide not to come anymore. So, it's better to leave the plan rather than keeping someone waiting for you. 


**2. UC Davis-only sign-in)**
- **What we heard:** When the interviewees did't see a sign-up page they assumed that the app was open to anyone and mentioned that would only use the app if it was only exclusive to UCD students.
- **What we changed:** The app now starts with "Sign in with UC Davis Google," and only @ucdavis.edu accounts can get in. The app is also connected to our live team server, so events people post are shared with everyone.
- **Why:** Being UC Davis-only is a big part of why students would trust and use the app, and it now shows from the very first screen.
