## Interview 3

**Interviewer:** Haylie Tan
**Note Taker:** Evelyn Xiao
**Interviewee:** Joey Mai
**Date:** 11/10/26
**Recording:** [Recording](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lsXvpHIEezwLgP6P3uG5rQj755SqjnIL/view?usp=drive_link)

### Pre-Interview Hypotheses
- We think most students find out about events through Instagram or word of mouth from friends. Since there are so many different social media pages posting events, it can be hard to find everything in one place. We also think students sometimes miss out on events because they hear about them too late or don't have enough time to plan with friends.

### Interview Notes

**1. Can you tell me about the last event or activity you attended around UC Davis or Davis?**
- Pre-health conference

**2. What was the event, and how did you first hear about it?**
- Heard about it through friends
- Nursing school students and information sessions about other schools

**3. Think about the last time you were looking for something to do. How did you go about finding it? (What sources did you use?)**
- Usually looks for things to do before weekends or days off
- Feels like Davis is a small town with not much to do
- Looks through Instagram pages

**4. Can you remember a time when you struggled to find something interesting to do?**
- Beginning of the quarter or school year
- Hard to know where to find event information
- Too many different social media pages
- Sometimes discovers pages she's never seen before
- Event posts don't show up on her feed until after the event has passed

**5. Tell me about the last time you saw an event you were interested in but decided not to go.**
- Raveyard
- Heard about it too late
- Would have needed more time to plan with friends

**6. What stopped you from attending?**
- Found out about the event too late to make plans

**7. Can you walk me through how you kept track of an event you wanted to attend recently?**
- Takes screenshots of event posts

**Follow-up: Did you save the post, take a screenshot, add it to your calendar, or use something else?**
- Screenshots

### Post-Interview Synthesis

- From this interview, we found that students mainly rely on Instagram and friends to find out about events, but it can be frustrating because there are so many different pages to keep up with. Sometimes events don't even show up on their feed until they've already happened. One thing that stood out was that the interviewee missed an event she was interested in because she found out about it too late and couldn't plan with her friends. She also mentioned that she usually just screenshots event posts to remember them. Overall, it seems like students want an easier way to find events ahead of time so they can actually make plans to go.

## Interview 4

**Interviewer:** Evelyn Xiao
**Note Taker:** Haylie Tan
**Interviewee:** Grace Macasaet
**Date:** 11/10/26
**Recording:** [Recording](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w8PeX5WMO6rBEOi42n-7CcY-rMIEu_HH/view?usp=drive_link)

### Pre-Interview Hypotheses
- We think most students find out about events through Instagram, friends, or clubs they're already involved in. Since event info is spread across different platforms, it can be hard to find things to do or keep track of upcoming events. We also think students are more likely to go to events if they have friends going with them.

**1. Can you tell me about the last event or activity you attended around UC Davis or Davis?**
- BSLS reveal night for the Filipino club "Mga Kapatid"
- People sign up to be mentors or mentees, and reveal night is when they find out who they're paired with

**2. What was the event, and how did you first hear about it?**
- First heard about the club four years ago when she joined
- Originally found out about the club through a friend
- Learned about the event through Instagram

**3. Think about the last time you were looking for something to do. How did you go about finding it? (What sources did you use?)**
- Uses Instagram for the most up-to-date event information
- Checks newsletters and email chains
- Also uses Discord

**4. Can you remember a time when you struggled to find something interesting to do?**
- Winter quarter feels pretty empty in terms of events
- Fewer events happen during winter
- A lot of clubs spend winter preparing for bigger events in spring

**5. Tell me about the last time you saw an event you were interested in but decided not to go.**
- Film enthusiast club meeting

**6. What stopped you from attending?**
- The meeting ended really late, around 10 PM
- Didn't feel safe going alone that late at night
- Would have gone if she had someone she trusted to go with

**7. Can you walk me through how you kept track of an event you wanted to attend recently?**
- Usually double-checks Instagram or other social media pages for event information

**8. Did you save the post, take a screenshot, add it to your calendar, or use something else?**
- Sometimes just makes a mental note
- Also uses Google Calendar to keep track of events

### Post-Interview Synthesis
From this interview, we found that Instagram is one of the main ways students keep up with events, but they also use Discord and emails. One interesting thing was that even if someone wants to go to an event, they might skip it if it's too late or they don't have anyone to go with. We also learned that students don't always have a good way to keep track of events and sometimes just rely on remembering them or checking Instagram again. Overall, it seems like finding events isn't the only issue, but also having people to go with and actually remembering when things are happening.
