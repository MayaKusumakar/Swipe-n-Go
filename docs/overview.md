# Swipe-N-Go Overview

## What problem are you solving and why is it important?
Information about school events, activities, and opportunities happening around the UC Davis campus is incredibly scattered. College students must sift through different platforms like Instagram, Discord, club websites, to stay up to date with different clubs. As a result, students often miss out on events due to not having a single reliable source to inform them of what is going on. 

On top of this, even if students find an event they are interested in, they might hesitate to attend if they do not know anyone else going. This can make it hard for new students, transfers, or anyone looking to meet new people. 

Our goal is to make discovering events easier, personalized, and intuitive. We to create an app that streamlines information about all UC Davis events and make it easier for students to connect with each other.
