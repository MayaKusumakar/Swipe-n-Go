# Swipe-N-Go Overview

## What problem are you solving and why is it important?
Information about school events, activities, and opportunities happening around the UC Davis campus is incredibly scattered. College students must sift through different platforms like Instagram, Discord, club websites, to stay up to date with different clubs. As a result, students often miss out on events due to not having a single reliable source to inform them of what is going on. 

On top of this, even if students find an event they are interested in, they might hesitate to attend if they do not know anyone else going. This can make it hard for new students, transfers, or anyone looking to meet new people. 

Our goal is to make discovering events easier, personalized, and intuitive. We to create an app that streamlines information about all UC Davis events and make it easier for students to connect with each other.

## How have others solved this problem and what is wrong with these solutions?
A lot of campus organizations have Instagram accounts, Discord channels, and Timetree's that display different information. While they do provide information about these events aspect, none of them fully combine event discovery with meeting new people.

- Instagram: events can get buried among unrelated content, and you must follow accounts to stay up to date.
- University Bullet-in Boards: Not all campus events get posted, a lot of the information can overlap and become convoluted.
- Discord: Primarily meant to be a chatting interface, information can get buried under messages.

## What is different about your solution and why does it solve the problem?
Swipe-N-Go is a campus focused event discover app that also doubles as a social media platform. It helps students find events that interest them based on a personalized algorithm. There will also be a heatmap that details in real time what is going on nearby. 

Our app will consist of 2 primary experiences:
1. Consumer App (Students): Students can discover campus events through a swipe-based interface. For instance, swiping right indicates that they're interested in the event and lift means they would skip that event. They can further filter events through different categories, interests, locations, and dates. On top of browsing events, students can explore a heatmap based on activity to see nearby events. Events will also showcase people who expressed their interest in going, so that prospective attendees can connect with those individuals.

2. Producer App (Club and Event Organizers): Student organizations, clubs, and local businesses can create and manage events. From event details to locations, these organizers can promote their activities and view engagement metrics (views, saves, RSVPs). 

## What are the key measures of success for your app (or in other words, how will you know that it actually solves the problem)?
We will determine the success of our app through the following methods:
- Event participation: How many students RSVP or save events they discovered through our application?
- Organizer engagement: How intuitive and easy it is for organizers to create events and reach their intended audiences?
- User satisfaction: Feedback from user testing, including whether students find teh app intuitive, useful, and worth using.
